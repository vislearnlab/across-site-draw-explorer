Across-Site Drawing Explorer

4,387 children's line drawings of 12 object categories, produced by kids aged 4–9 at four international sites — ■ San Jose (USA), ■ Beijing (China), ■ New Delhi (India), ■ Kisumu (Kenya). Each drawing sits in a 2-D map of its CLIP (ViT-B/32) embedding. Hover a point for the drawing; click to pin and replay the strokes. Tablet-site drawings re-rendered crisply from raw strokes; Kisumu is scanned on paper.
